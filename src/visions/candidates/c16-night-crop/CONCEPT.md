# CONCEPT — Night Crop

- **id:** `c16`
- **slug:** `night-crop`
- **title:** Night Crop
- **status:** `motion`

## Thesis
Prime is shot like a fashion night — vertical scroll recrops the frame until each room is the only thing in the finder.

## Mood
editorial, kinetic, gloss, after-hours, sharp, alive

## Scroll grammar
Sticky finder-crop: vertical scroll whip-pans a wide night strip through a fixed viewport crop; each beat hard-locks a new recrop.

## Layout grammar
Type lives as overlaid crop marks and edge captions; rooms are full-bleed frames inside the finder; brand sits as a corner filename stamp.

## Hero
- **Composition:** Full-bleed finder with corner crop marks, a wide night plane waiting off-axis, `PRIME.RAW` stamped top-left, shot-count ticking bottom-right.
- **Phrases:** “Crop the night.” / “Stay in the finder.”

## Structure
1. **Hero** — finder opens on the street threshold; Prime is a filename, not a logo lockup.
2. **Crop Heat** — recrop onto bar + kitchen tungsten; steam and glass in the frame.
3. **Crop Play** — recrop onto the arena; screens smear, then lock.
4. **Crop Voice** — recrop onto karaoke; a hot mic in a tight crop.
5. **Crop Screen** — recrop onto cinema; the frame becomes the movie.
6. **Crop Close** — recrop onto lodge hush; the finder closes on ember.

## Motion signature
1. Whip-pan of the night strip (GSAP) — horizontal `x` of the wide strip is scrubbed by vertical scroll; each beat slam-locks.
2. Crop-mark tick + exposure flash (GSAP) — marks snap inward on lock with a one-frame white flash.
3. EXIF caption scramble then settle (GSAP) — shot notes decrypt as each crop holds.

## Type
- **Display:** Syne ExtraBold, huge, flush to crop edges, tight tracking — fashion-poster scale
- **Body:** IBM Plex Mono, 11–13px, technical captions like EXIF / shot notes

## Palette cue
Void black `#08080C`, paper white `#F3EFE6`, acid lime `#C8F542`, magenta flash `#FF3D8A`, cool chrome `#B8BEC8`, tungsten `#E8A86A`. High-contrast editorial, not gold luxury.

## Non-goals
- No gentlemen’s-club gold serif
- No long manifesto copy
- No soft cross-fades between rooms
- No photo-real rooms until curated assets

## Refs
- Fashion-night crop language; Prime as a living still.
