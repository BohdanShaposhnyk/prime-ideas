## Thesis
Moody weather outside the terrace glass, then you descend into Prime — richer, darker, more intimate room by room.

## Mood
weather-threshold, terrace-glass, asymmetric type, rich texture, deepen-inward

## Hero
- **Composition:** full-bleed two-plane window — bottom-left near: Norwegian-rich interior texture; top-right far: fresh moody sky/weather; look-out-from-terrace depth
- **Phrases:** “PRIME.” / “Come in from the weather.”

## Structure
1. **Hero** — two-plane terrace/window; huge/tiny asymmetric type; weather distant, interior in front
2. **Panorama** — open restaurant with panoramic windows; sky still present, space opens
3. **Game room** — darker, denser, screen-lit comfort
4. **Hookah | bar** — deepest warm/comfy interior; soft close CTA

## Motion signature
1. Scroll-depth parallax (GSAP) — weather plane recedes; interior plane advances / “enter”
2. Type contrast (GSAP) — oversized brand vs tiny micro-line; staggered reveal
3. Atmosphere shift (GSAP) — palette cools→warms and darkens as sections go inward

## Type
- **Display:** extreme scale contrast — massive brand vs whisper caption; asymmetric placement
- **Body:** clean modern sans; short lines only

## Palette cue
Cool slate/mist weather; warm wood + velvet interior; deep charcoal rooms with amber accents

## Non-goals
- No amenity card grids or stat strips in hero
- No floating badges / promo chips on media
- No flat single-plane hero (must keep two-depth window read)
