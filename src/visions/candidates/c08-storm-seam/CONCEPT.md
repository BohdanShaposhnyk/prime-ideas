# CONCEPT — Storm Seam

- **id:** `c08`
- **slug:** `storm-seam`
- **title:** Storm Seam
- **status:** `motion`

## Thesis
A storm sky opens into a Norwegian lodge bar, then a corner seam rips the night into neon dual panels, a black spiral of four rooms, and the side floor.

## Mood
storm sky, lodge warmth, diagonal seam, neon aggression, spiral cards

## Scroll grammar
Pinned non-vertical runway: 2.5D hero→lodge, then straight 40°-from-vertical seam slides BR→center into two triangles with opposite seam-aligned page scrolls; spiral pin; lateral floor.

## Layout grammar
Huge–tiny asymmetric overflow type; brand as a migrating masthead plane across seams; media full-bleed until the seam splits it.

## Hero
- **Composition:** Full-bleed abstract night — layered lightning veins, star scatter, cold depth planes; PRIME oversized and clipped by the frame edge.
- **Phrases:** “The sky cracks first.” / “Then the lodge holds.”

## Structure
1. **Hero** — Abstract nature / lightning / stars; 2.5D parallax layers (inert hooks).
2. **Lodge** — Full-screen premium wood / stone / greenery / glass; left rail block 1.
3. **Bar** — Left rail block 2 (revealed scrolling BL).
4. **Stage** — Right rail block 1 after seam rolls onto lodge.
5. **Gaming** — Right rail block 2 / arena (revealed scrolling TR).
6. **Spiral** — Revealed as dual curtains fly apart along the seam.
7. **Floor** — Lateral side-floor finale after the four cards.

## Motion signature
1. 2.5D parallax hero → lodge on one continuous pin (GSAP).
2. Straight 40° seam rolls BR→center; lodge|stage split, then lodge→bar (BL) and stage→gaming (TR).
3. Curtains continue along the seam and fly off, revealing InfiniteSpiral behind; then floor.

## Type
- **Display:** Unbounded — huge / tiny clash, asymmetric overflow, edge-clipped mastheads
- **Body:** Space Grotesk — tight supporting lines under migrating captions

Neon dual + spiral: pure black `#050505`, acid `#B8FF3C`, hot magenta `#FF2D6A`, stark white `#F5F5F5`.
