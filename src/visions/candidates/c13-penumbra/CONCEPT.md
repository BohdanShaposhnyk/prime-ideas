# CONCEPT — Penumbra

- **id:** `c13`
- **slug:** `penumbra`
- **title:** Penumbra
- **status:** `motion`

## Thesis
Prime is found in a moving rake of light — scroll pulls focus through warm shadow until each room snaps in the penumbra.

## Mood
chiaroscuro, optical, amber, hush, depth, specular

## Scroll grammar
Sticky light-rake — each beat pins a depth stack; scroll rakes a diagonal key-light band through near / mid / far planes so rooms are found by light, not by travel.

## Layout grammar
Type-as-lit-architecture — monumental display type occupies the lit face, flush left and oversized; rooms are soft color masses in the crush; brand is a hairline specular on the right; CTA lives in the penumbra band.

## Hero
- **Composition:** Crushed void. Volumetric LightRays from upper-left. A diagonal ivory rake cuts the frame; “PRIME” sits half in the band, half in shadow, with a soft ghost in the far plane. Specular hairline on the right rim.
- **Phrases:** “Found by light.” / “Stay in the band.”

## Structure
1. **Hero** — key light finds the name
2. **Heat plane** — kitchen / bar as a warm mass entering the rake
3. **Play plane** — gaming as cool fill on the unlit side
4. **Voice plane** — karaoke as a single rose highlight
5. **Haze plane** — hookah as volumetric bloom
6. **Dark plane** — a single bright shard in crushed black
7. **Close** — rake opens; afterglow holds

## Motion signature
1. Focal rake (GSAP) — blur / brightness of near·mid·far planes tied to distance from the moving light band
2. Type catch (GSAP) — display glyphs pick up specular and tighten tracking as they enter the rake
3. Bokeh drift (GSAP) — near-plane orbs parallax and stay over-blurred

## Type
- **Display:** Bodoni Moda — high-contrast fashion serif; huge, tight tracking, hairlines catching the rake
- **Body:** Sora — quiet labels, supporting lines, CTA

## Palette cue
