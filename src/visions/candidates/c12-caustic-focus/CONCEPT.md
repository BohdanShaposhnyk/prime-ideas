# CONCEPT — Caustic Focus

- **id:** `c12`
- **slug:** `caustic-focus`
- **title:** Caustic Focus
- **status:** `motion`

## Thesis
Prime is seen through a cinema lens — scroll racks the focal plane through liquid glass until each room snaps sharp.

## Mood
cinematic, caustic, bokeh, liquid-glass, shadow-play, type-discovery

## Scroll grammar
Vertical rack-focus — a fixed optical axis holds the viewport; scroll drives a focal plane down stacked glass plates so type and rooms snap from bokeh to sharp only when they hit the plane.

## Layout grammar
Centered lens axis — oversized display type sits on the glass as the subject; rooms are stacked focal plates behind it, with liquid caustics in the middle and shadow falloff at the rim.

## Hero
- **Composition:** Dark cinema iris looking into liquid glass. A huge serif line starts as blown bokeh, then racks onto the optical axis while caustic light crawls the pane.
- **Phrases:** “Rack the night.” / “Prime in focus.”

## Structure
1. **Hero** — lens looking into liquid glass; type discovers from blur
2. **Heat plate** — kitchen racks in on warm caustics
3. **Play plate** — gaming arena collapses from neon bokeh to sharp
4. **Screen plate** — cinema letterbox, deepest shadow
5. **Voice plate** — karaoke; type echoes in the glass as it hits focus
6. **Close plate** — hookah linger; smoke-liquid, iris rest

## Motion signature
1. Focal-plane rack (GSAP) — plates blur / sharpen / scale as they cross the optical axis
2. Type discovery (GSAP word spans) — glyphs unblur and settle only when their plate is in focus
3. Liquid caustic drift (GSAP) — glass caustics and specular crawl with the rack

## Type
- **Display:** Instrument Serif — optical-size huge on the glass; italic for discovery lines
- **Body:** Outfit — quiet labels, room whispers, focus marks
