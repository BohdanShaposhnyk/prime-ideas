# CONCEPT — Film Gate

- **id:** `c04`
- **slug:** `film-gate`
- **title:** Film Gate
- **status:** `structure`

## Thesis
Prime framed like cinema stock — each gate holds a room until the night advances one frame.

## Mood
cinematic, taut, nocturnal, electric, intimate

## Scroll grammar
film-gate — sticky aperture window with fixed sprocket rails; successive frames pin and replace inside the gate as you scroll

## Layout grammar
brand locked in the gate top bar; headline + CTA in the lower third of the aperture; media fills the gate edge-to-edge behind type

## Hero
- **Composition:** Full-bleed aperture between ink rails; PRIME stamped across the top bar; cyan–amber mesh plane filling the frame.
- **Phrases:** “One night. One reel.” / “Advance the frame.”

## Structure
1. **Hero** — first frame open on brand
2. **Arena** — cyber floor pulse in the gate
3. **Screen** — cinema projection warmth
4. **Mic** — karaoke stage heat
5. **Ember** — lounge hush / late linger

## Motion signature
1. Gate pin + frame-advance scrub (GSAP) — sticky frames replace in the aperture
2. Sprocket tick — rail marks pulse per frame change
3. Aperture breathe — soft scale on enter

## Type
- **Display:** Teko — condensed film-title scale, locked to the gate bar
- **Body:** Karla — captions, supporting line, CTA

## Palette cue
Ink `oklch(0.09 0.012 45)`, rail silver `oklch(0.48 0.01 90)`, arena cyan `oklch(0.74 0.11 195)`, screen amber `oklch(0.78 0.13 75)`, mic coral `oklch(0.68 0.14 25)`, ember charcoal `oklch(0.28 0.04 50)`, chalk `oklch(0.93 0.01 95)`

## Non-goals
- No purple club-neon look
- No amenity card grids or schedule strips
- No horizontal carousel — frames advance vertically in the sticky gate
- No detached badges on media
