# Prime Warsaw — Overview

All-in-one Warsaw night venue: gaming, cinema, karaoke, hookah, bar, kitchen. Premium lounge + cyber arena under one roof.

## Brand
- **Handle:** [@prime_warsaw](https://www.instagram.com/prime_warsaw/)
- **Telegram:** [t.me/primewarsaw](https://t.me/primewarsaw)
- **Mood:** dark, expensive, moody; warm lounge light vs cool gaming neon; crown “PRIME” mark
- **Refs:** local moodboard in `instagram_ref/` (reference only — do not import into the app unless curated into `src/assets`)

## Product priorities
- **Wow-effect over content** — technically impressive design first
- **Themes client likes:** lightning / nature / stars
- **Copy:** English only; minimum text; catchy simple phrases (typography as eye candy)
- **Layout:** mobile-first
